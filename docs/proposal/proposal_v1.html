01 · Executive summary

Stop paying the ghost before the money leaves the account

All criteria

The problem. Philippine businesses pay suppliers on documents that are easy to fake. The Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) has filed cases alleging billions of pesos in tax losses from "ghost" corporations that exist only to sell fictitious receipts, and from the companies that bought them. Globally, 68% of organizations surveyed by Basware saw at least one accounts-payable (AP) fraud attempt in 2024. Most Philippine SMEs have no fraud team; AP review is a clerk looking at a PDF.

The solution. InvoiceSentinel is a pre-payment risk desk. An analyst uploads an invoice image or PDF; Gemini 3.8 Flash reads the document directly (no OCR templates), returns structured fields and line items, and raises explainable risk flags with quoted evidence: ghost vendor, bank-change / business-email-compromise (BEC) payment redirection, 12% VAT math errors, overbilling, duplicates, template-looking documents, missing fields and amount mismatches. A deterministic control layer re-checks the arithmetic and enforces score floors. Each invoice becomes a case in a severity-ranked inbox where a human decides: Approve, Escalate or Request docs, with every step written to an audit trail.

Status. A working end-to-end prototype is live on Cloud Run in asia-southeast1, with the Gemini key in Secret Manager. In an 8-invoice test round of Philippine-style samples, every case landed in its expected severity band after calibration (legitimate invoices 0–5; ghost vendor 95–98; bank-change 95).

Scope, stated plainly: InvoiceSentinel is AP risk triage before payment. It does not certify BIR compliance, is not a tax-authority system, and never auto-rejects. A person makes every payment decision.

Why this matters for a finance team

  • The documents look real. Ghost receipts carry a TIN, a VAT line and a clean letterhead. The BIR describes sellers as corporations with no operations, employees or assets, set up only to issue receipts [3].
  • The cost lands on the buyer. In the 2025 cases it was the purchasing companies, their officers and their accountants who faced complaints [3]. A fake expense is cash out the door, followed by audit exposure.
  • Payment-redirection scams hit the same desk. A "new bank account, please don't call" notice on an otherwise normal invoice can drain an SME account in one transfer.
  • SMEs review by eye. Without a fraud team, the control is whoever opens the PDF. Basware's survey points to reliance on manual processes as a key gap [7].

How we use these numbers

Caveats we keep: the BIR figures are allegations in complaints and filings, not final court findings. They measure tax revenue loss, not AP losses. The 68% figure comes from a global survey of 100 respondents, not a Philippine one. We use them as motivating context for stronger private-sector controls. InvoiceSentinel does not investigate or relate to these specific cases.

The pattern behind them is what we design for. A plausible-looking supplier document combines weak vendor substance (placeholder or unknown TIN, vague "facilitation fee" lines) with numbers that don't hold up, and it gets paid because no one had time to check. Those signals are visible on the document itself, so a multimodal model can read for them at the point of payment.

Alignment with the BFSI problem statement

The theme asks for AI that addresses "detecting and mitigating fraud or risk, automating complex processes" across financial services [10]. InvoiceSentinel does both:

  • Fraud & risk detection: ghost vendors, BEC payment redirection, duplicates and overbilling are named fraud typologies, each a first-class flag.
  • Process automation: manual keying and arithmetic checks become a ~20-second multimodal read plus deterministic verification.
  • Financial controls: the AP-to-payment step is the last control before cash moves. That makes it relevant to banks (payment fraud), insurers (claims documents use the same pattern) and corporate finance.

04 · The solution, end to end

Upload → Gemini reads and reasons → human decides → audit trail

GenAI 40%UX 10%
STEP 1UploadDrag in a PDF, PNG or JPG: a scan, a supplier PDF or a phone photo.
STEP 2 · GEMINIExtract + reasonVendor, TIN, invoice #, date, subtotal, VAT, total and line items, plus risk flags with explanation and evidence.
STEP 3 · CONTROLSVerify + score12% VAT recompute, line math, exact-duplicate check, severity score floor → 0–100 score.
STEP 4 · HUMANDecideSeverity-ranked inbox. Approve, Escalate to fraud/finance, or Request docs, with an optional note.
STEP 5 · RECORDAudit + monitorTimestamped trail per case; dashboard shows ₱ at risk, open, approved and escalated.

Design principles

  • Triage, not verdicts. The score orders the queue; it never blocks or pays anything by itself.
  • Explain every flag. Each risk card shows a code, severity, a plain-English explanation and the evidence it relied on, often quoted from the document.
  • Context beyond one document. Gemini sees the recent case history, so it can recognize a known vendor's TIN and spot near-duplicates.
  • Never fake an answer. If Gemini is unavailable after retries, the API returns a retryable error and the UI offers Retry. It never invents a "clean" case.
  • Honest positioning. A disclaimer on every case page: supports AP triage; does not certify BIR compliance.

05 · How Gemini is used (1/2)

One multimodal call replaces OCR, data entry and first-pass review

GenAI 40%Innovation 25%

The analysis call

  1. Input: the raw file bytes (image or PDF) sent as a multimodal part to gemini-3.8-flash through the google-genai SDK. No OCR engine, no per-vendor template.
  2. Context: the prompt includes a compact list of up to 40 prior cases (vendor, invoice #, total, date, TIN), so the model can reason across documents, not only within one.
  3. Contract: response_mime_type="application/json", temperature 0.2, and an explicit schema: fields, line items, a 1–2 sentence summary, risk_flags[] (code, severity, title, explanation, evidence) and overall_risk_score.
  4. Resilience: up to 4 attempts with backoff on 429 / 503 / UNAVAILABLE / RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED. After that, an explicit "analysis unavailable" error. There is no mock fallback in live mode.
  5. Normalization: output is parsed, type-checked and passed to the deterministic control layer (next page) before it becomes a case.

Calibration rules in the prompt

The first test round showed the usual LLM failure modes: false alarms on plain layouts and blurry photos, and fraud labels on the wrong code. We wrote explicit PH-specific rules into the prompt:

  • A complete, consistent invoice (real-looking TIN, address, ATP/series, correct 12% VAT) should score 0–20 with no flags. A plain or computer-generated layout alone is not a risk.
  • Image quality is not fraud. If a field is unreadable, lower confidence in the summary; use MISSING_FIELDS only for fields that are actually absent.
  • Small non-VAT vendor receipts without customer TIN or terms are normal in the Philippines.
  • Unit prices far above typical PH market prices → OVERBILLING, with the typical range as evidence. AMOUNT_MISMATCH is only for totals that don't add up.
  • New or different bank account, a personal name or e-wallet, or "don't call us" → UNUSUAL_PAYMENT_CHANGE (high or critical).
  • Same vendor, same total and a similar date but a different invoice # → DUPLICATE_INVOICE (near-duplicate).
  • A TIN that matches an existing case is a known vendor; don't flag its TIN pattern as a ghost.
Why GenAI is essential here: the strongest fraud signals are semantic. Examples are a "facilitation fee" with no deliverable, a payment notice telling staff not to call, and ₱2,450 for a ream of bond paper. Rules and OCR can't see these; a multimodal model reads them the way a senior AP reviewer would, then shows its evidence.

06 · How Gemini is used (2/2)

Eight explainable risk signals, backed by code where math allows

Flag codeWhat Gemini looks for on the documentDeterministic layer (code, after Gemini)
GHOST_VENDORPlaceholder or implausible TIN (e.g., 000-000-000-000), thin vendor substance, vague single-line services, unexplained high totalsKnown-vendor rule: prompt receives prior TINs, so a matching TIN isn't flagged; severity floor applies

MATH_ERRORArithmetic inconsistencies the model notices while extractingVAT recomputed: flags if VAT ≠ 12% × VATable sales (tolerance max(₱1, 1%))
AMOUNT_MISMATCHTotals that don't reconcile with line itemsLines + VAT vs total (2% tolerance); VAT-inclusive receipts accepted when lines = total
OVERBILLINGUnit prices far above typical PH market prices; cites the expected rangeMis-coded "overpriced / above market" mismatches re-coded to OVERBILLING
DUPLICATE_INVOICENear-duplicates: same vendor and total, similar date, different invoice #Exact match on vendor + invoice # + total against the case store
TEMPLATE_LOOKINGGeneric online-template look combined with missing substance (never layout alone)Severity floor applies
MISSING_FIELDSAbsent address, remittance details, TIN, ATP/series. Not unreadable fields on a blurry photoSeverity floor applies

Why hybrid

LLMs are strong readers and weak calculators. Gemini does what only it can do (read any layout, judge semantics, quote evidence); code does what must be exact (tax math, exact duplicates, score monotonicity).

Score floor by severity

The overall score can never read lower than the worst flag implies (low 25 · medium 45 · high 65 · critical 85). A critical flag can't be buried by an optimistic model score.

Severity bands

low 0–39 · medium 40–64 · high 65–84 · critical 85–100. The bands drive inbox order and the "₱ at risk" KPI (high + critical open totals).

07 · Architecture on Google Cloud

Serverless, region-local and ready to scale from zero

Technical merit 40%

Deployed today

Two Cloud Run services in asia-southeast1: the Next.js web, and the FastAPI API (1 vCPU / 512 Mi, 0–2 instances, 120 s timeout). The Gemini API key is stored in Secret Manager and mounted on the API. The web build runs on Cloud Build, because the API URL is inlined at build time. A billing budget alert guards cost.

Honest MVP limits

Cases live in a JSON store inside the container. They are seeded on start and ephemeral, so with two instances the case lists can briefly differ. There is no authentication yet. Both are deliberate prototype shortcuts, and both have a mapped production path.

Production path

Firestore for cases and audit events, Cloud Storage for invoice files (signed URLs), Firebase Auth / Identity Platform for analyst vs approver roles, the Gemini Batch API for bulk back-office runs (50% lower token price [8]), and Cloud Run autoscaling for peaks at month-end close.

What changed to get here

  • Calibrated prompt rules for legitimate PH documents, blur and non-VAT receipts (cut false positives on 01, 02 and 08).
  • Deterministic 12% VAT check and VAT-inclusive total handling (made 04 reliable).
  • Dedicated OVERBILLING code instead of a generic mismatch (06).
  • Severity score floor, so a critical flag can't sit at a low score.
  • Retries on 429/503, and removal of the silent mock fallback in live mode.

Performance and honest limits

  • Latency: about 10–34 s from upload to verdict, dominated by the Gemini call. That is fine for pre-payment review, not for point-of-sale.
  • Run-to-run variance: scores move a few points between runs (e.g., 45–65 on the VAT case) even at temperature 0.2. Bands and flags were stable; exact scores are not. The deterministic floor limits downside drift.
  • Small test set: 8 synthetic PH-style samples demonstrate behavior; they are not a statistical accuracy claim. A labeled evaluation set is on the roadmap.
  • Blurry photos: TIN digits can be misread, so the TIN-based checks are weaker on poor images.

Infrastructure

Cloud Run scales to zero, so an idle desk costs nothing. At prototype and SME volumes, usage stays within Cloud Run's monthly free tier [9], and a US$8 (~₱500) budget alert with 50/90/100% thresholds protects the billing account. Costs become material only at sustained scale. By then the per-invoice model cost dominates, and it is still under a peso.

Scalability

  • Stateless API: horizontal autoscaling on Cloud Run. The only state moves to Firestore and Cloud Storage on the roadmap.
  • Throughput levers: Gemini Batch API for overnight bulk runs at half the token price [8]; Flash by default, with a Pro-tier second opinion only on high and critical cases.
  • Multi-tenant ready: case history is already passed per request, so tenant-scoped history is a data-layer change, not a model change.

Sustainability of the product

With model costs under ₱1 per invoice, a low monthly subscription for SMEs, or a per-seat price bundled by an accounting firm or bank SME program, can cover costs with margin. The same engine can serve banks and insurers as an API for supplier-payment and claims-document screening.

What is new

  • Cross-document reasoning: the model sees recent case history, so it recognizes known vendors and near-duplicates instead of judging each PDF in isolation.
  • PH-first signals: TIN patterns, 12% VAT, ATP/series, non-VAT receipts, GCash and personal-account payees, and peso market prices for overbilling.
  • Hybrid trust model: GenAI for judgment, code for arithmetic and score floors, a human for the decision.

User experience

  • Three clicks to a decision: inbox → case → Approve / Escalate / Request docs, with an optional note.
  • Readable at a glance: severity badges use text labels as well as color. Amounts are in ₱; timestamps are Manila time.
  • Familiar inputs: drag-and-drop or file picker for PDF, PNG and JPG up to 20 MB, including phone photos.
  • Transparent system state: the header shows the API status and model; failures show a clear error with Retry.

Responsible AI by design

  • Human-in-the-loop: no automated rejection or payment. The model ranks; people decide, and their decisions are logged.
  • Explainability: every flag carries an explanation and evidence that an analyst can check against the document.
  • No fabricated output: if the model is unavailable, the system says so and offers a retry instead of guessing.
  • Fairness to suppliers: small and non-VAT vendors are explicitly not penalized for normal informality, and blur is not treated as fraud.
  • Data handling: on the Gemini API paid tier, content is not used to improve Google's products [8]; production runs on the paid tier and adds signed URLs, upload scanning, rate limits and retention controls.
  • Clear scope: not a BIR tool, not tax advice, not a compliance certificate.
Key risks and mitigations: score variance → bands, floors and human review; adversarial "clean-looking" fakes → vendor master and bank-history checks on the roadmap; model or pricing changes → model name is configurable (GEMINI_MODEL) and the cost is tracked per invoice.
