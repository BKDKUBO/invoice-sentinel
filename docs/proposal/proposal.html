01The problem

Fake receipts are an industry, and accounts payable is where they get paid

Problem & impact

Ghost-receipt cases filed by the BIR (alleged, ₱ billions)

68% of organizations hit at least one AP fraud attempt in 2024; only 31% use automated fraud detection (Basware survey of 100 finance leaders [7]).

BIR figures are allegations in complaints and filings, not final court findings. Sources on page 13.

The documents look real

Ghost receipts carry a TIN, a VAT line and a clean letterhead. They come from corporations with no operations, set up only to issue receipts [3].

The buyer takes the hit

In the 2025 cases, purchasing companies, their officers and their CPAs faced complaints [3]. A fake expense means cash out the door, then audit exposure.

Bank-change scams hit the same desk

A "new bank account, please don't call" note on an otherwise normal invoice can empty an SME account in one transfer. Most SMEs have no fraud team; the only control is the clerk who opens the PDF.

02The solution

A pre-payment risk desk: upload, check, decide

GenAIUX

1Upload

Photo, scan or PDF, or one click on Try a sample.

›

2Gemini reads

Fields, line items, payee and bank details in structured JSON. No OCR templates.

›

3Code checks

Vendor master, PO, TIN, BIR RR 7-2024, split invoices, VAT math: deterministic rules.

›

4Agent explains

Ask the case in any language; the ADK agent cites evidence and drafts a verification email.

›

5Human decides

Approve, Escalate or Request docs. Every step goes to the audit trail.

Live dashboard: KPI cards, risk trend, flag breakdown and severity mix.

Design principles

  • Triage, not verdicts. The score orders the queue; it never pays or blocks anything by itself.
  • Evidence for every claim. Risk cards and agent answers cite evidence IDs (E1–E9, IMG) that the analyst can open.
  • Code for facts, AI for judgment. Matching, TIN checks, BIR rules and scoring are deterministic; Gemini reads the document and explains.
  • Never fake an answer. If Gemini is down, the API returns a retryable error. It never invents a clean case.
  • Honest scope. Supports AP triage; it does not certify BIR compliance.

03Theme fit

Built for BFSI: trust, fraud and risk at the last control before cash moves

Problem alignment

Fraud

Named fraud types are first-class flags: ghost vendor, bank-change / BEC payment redirection, duplicates, overbilling, split invoices that dodge approval limits.

Risk

A deterministic 0–100 score with severity floors ranks the inbox. Shows "₱ at risk" and high/critical open cases, so controllers can see their exposure at a glance.

Trust

Every flag is explained with evidence. The verification email goes only to the contact on file, never the one printed on the invoice. A person makes every payment decision.

Why it matters beyond one company

  • The theme asks for AI that detects and mitigates fraud or risk and automates complex financial processes [10]. InvoiceSentinel does both, before the payment goes out.
  • BIR RR 7-2024 invoice checks and TIN validation make it harder for ghost receipts to enter the books, which protects tax revenue.
  • A light browser tool rather than an ERP, so SMEs can adopt it without a fraud team.

04Features · controls

Deterministic checks catch what a tired reviewer misses

Technical meritInnovation

Vendor master & PO matching

Vendor, TIN, bank account, PO number, line quantities and prices vs the master. A changed bank account is a critical flag.

TIN validation

Format and branch-code checks; compares the vendor TIN with the master record.

BIR RR 7-2024 checklist

Required invoice elements with section references, buyer-TIN rule for VAT sales ≥ ₱1,000. Advisory only.

Split-invoice detection

Same vendor, close dates, combined total above the approval limit (set in Settings) → SPLIT_INVOICE.

Deterministic scoring

Severity weights (low 20 · medium 45 · high 70 · critical 90) +5 per extra flag. Same input, same score.

Gemini extraction

Multimodal read with a JSON schema at temperature 0, seed 7, with retries and a 60 s timeout.

06Architecture

Serverless on Google Cloud, end to end

Technical merit

Stateless services. Both containers autoscale on Cloud Run; all state lives in Firestore and Cloud Storage.

Secrets stay secret. The Gemini key is in Secret Manager, never in code or images. Uploads go to a private bucket.

One-command deploy. deploy.sh provisions Firestore, the bucket and IAM, then builds and deploys both services with Cloud Build.

09Community benefit & accessibility

Fraud protection for the businesses that have no fraud team

ImpactUX

SMEs

MSMEs are most businesses in the Philippines, and most have no AP controls beyond one person. A browser tool that works from a phone photo brings bank-grade checks within reach.

Tax revenue

Every ghost receipt that is stopped is a fake deduction and fake input VAT kept out of the books. BIR RR 7-2024 checks point clerks to the missing elements before payment.

Multilingual

Clerks ask in Tagalog, English or mixed Taglish and get answers and supplier emails in that language, so staff and suppliers both understand them.

Accessibility built in

  • WCAG AA contrast for body text; severity is shown as text labels, not only color
  • Visible gold focus rings and full keyboard navigation; aria-current in the nav
  • ARIA labels on inputs, live regions for agent progress, reduced-motion support
  • Responsive layouts tested at phone width (390 px)

Responsible by design

  • Human in the loop: no automatic rejection or payment
  • Small and non-VAT vendors are not penalized for normal informality; blur is not treated as fraud
  • Emails are only drafted; the recipient comes from the master record
  • Paid-tier Gemini API: content is not used to train Google's models [8]
