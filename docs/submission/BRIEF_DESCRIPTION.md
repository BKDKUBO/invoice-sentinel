# Brief description (≈130 words)

InvoiceSentinel is a pre-payment fraud and risk desk for Philippine SMEs. It catches ghost receipts, bank-change scams, duplicates, overbilling and split invoices before money leaves the account. **Gemini 3.8 Flash** reads invoice photos and PDFs into structured JSON. Deterministic code then checks each invoice against the vendor master, purchase orders, TIN rules and BIR RR 7-2024, and scores the risk. A **Google ADK** agent answers questions in English or Tagalog, streams each tool step live, cites evidence, recommends PAY, HOLD or VERIFY, and drafts verification emails to the contact on file. **Firebase Hosting** serves the app at invoicesentinel.web.app and forwards requests to the Next.js web service on **Cloud Run**. A FastAPI service, also on **Cloud Run**, runs the checks and the agent. **Firestore** stores cases, vendors, POs, chats and audit trails. **Cloud Storage** keeps the uploaded invoices private. **Secret Manager** holds the Gemini key. A person makes every payment decision.
